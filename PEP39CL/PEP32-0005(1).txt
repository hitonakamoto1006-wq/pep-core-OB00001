PEP32-0005 HD Wallet

Version: 0.1 Draft

===========================================================

1.  Abstract

This specification defines the architecture of a Hierarchical
Deterministic (HD) Wallet within the PEP ecosystem.

An HD Wallet SHALL deterministically derive all wallet objects from a
single Master Seed.

===========================================================

2.  Goals

-   Single Backup
-   Deterministic
-   Hierarchical
-   Chain Independent
-   Extensible
-   Secure

===========================================================

3.  Architecture

PEP39CL Mnemonic ↓ Entropy ↓ Master Seed ↓ Chain Adapter ↓ Derivation
Path ↓ Extended Keys ↓ Accounts ↓ Addresses

===========================================================

4.  Wallet Model

An HD Wallet SHALL:

-   Own exactly one Master Seed.
-   Support multiple Chain Adapters.
-   Support multiple Accounts.
-   Support deterministic Address generation.
-   Preserve hierarchical structure.

===========================================================

5.  Recovery

Restoring the same PEP39CL mnemonic SHALL recreate the same HD Wallet.

No chain-specific recovery phrase is required.

===========================================================

6.  Watch-only

Implementations MAY create watch-only wallets using Extended Public
Keys.

Watch-only wallets SHALL NOT sign transactions.

===========================================================

7.  Security Considerations

Implementations SHOULD:

-   Protect the Master Seed.
-   Protect Extended Private Keys.
-   Erase temporary secrets after use.
-   Never expose sensitive material unnecessarily.

===========================================================

8.  Compatibility

PEP32 SHALL remain independent of blockchain-specific wallet
implementations.

Support for additional blockchains SHALL be provided through Chain
Adapters.

===========================================================
