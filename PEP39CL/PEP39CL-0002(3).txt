PEP39CL-0002 Wordlist Specification

Version: 0.1 Draft

===========================================================

1.  Abstract

This document defines the wordlist used by PEP39CL.

The wordlist provides a deterministic mapping between binary values and
human-readable words.

This specification does not define the encoding algorithm.

===========================================================

2.  Goals

-   Human-readable
-   Easy to pronounce
-   Easy to distinguish
-   Deterministic
-   Language-independent
-   Forward compatible

===========================================================

3.  Word Requirements

Each word:

-   MUST be unique.
-   MUST contain only lowercase letters (a-z).
-   MUST NOT contain spaces.
-   MUST NOT contain digits.
-   MUST NOT contain punctuation.
-   MUST NOT contain accented characters.
-   SHOULD be easy to pronounce.
-   SHOULD avoid visually similar spellings.

===========================================================

4.  Word Length

Minimum Length: 3 characters Maximum Length: 8 characters Recommended
Length: 4-7 characters

===========================================================

5.  Wordlist Size

4096 words.

Each word SHALL have a fixed numeric index in the range 0-4095.

===========================================================

6.  Word Ordering

-   Words SHALL have a fixed ordering.
-   The numeric index SHALL NEVER change.
-   New words SHALL NOT be inserted between existing words.
-   Future revisions MAY append new words.

===========================================================

7.  Reserved Words

No reserved words are defined in Version 1.

===========================================================

8.  Localization

The canonical wordlist SHALL be English.

Localized wordlists MAY be defined in future specifications.

All localized wordlists SHALL preserve the same word index.

===========================================================

9.  Compatibility

Implementations SHALL use the canonical word index.

Implementations SHALL NOT reorder or modify the wordlist.

===========================================================
