PEP39CL-0001 PEP-chain Mnemonic Standard

Version : 0.1 Draft Status : Draft

===========================================================

1.  Abstract

PEP39CL defines the mnemonic format used by the PEP ecosystem.

Its purpose is to encode binary entropy into a human-readable mnemonic
and decode it back without loss.

PEP39CL does NOT define wallet derivation, key generation, or
blockchain-specific algorithms.

Those responsibilities belong to PEP32 and Chain Adapters.

===========================================================

2.  Goals

-   Human-readable
-   Deterministic
-   Blockchain Independent
-   Cryptography Independent
-   Forward Compatible
-   Error Detectable
-   Easy Backup

===========================================================

3.  Non Goals

PEP39CL does NOT define:

-   Wallets
-   Private Keys
-   Public Keys
-   Addresses
-   Signatures
-   HD Wallet
-   Blockchain Derivation
-   Chain-specific Formats
-   Chain-specific Recovery Phrases

===========================================================

4.  Terminology

Entropy Random binary generated by a Cryptographically Secure Random
Number Generator (CSPRNG).

Mnemonic Human-readable representation of entropy.

Word One logical unit inside the mnemonic.

Master Seed Binary data generated by PEP32.

Adapter Module converting the Master Seed into chain-specific keys.

===========================================================

5.  Design Principles

6.  PEP39CL SHALL be blockchain-independent.

7.  PEP39CL SHALL be cryptography-independent.

8.  PEP39CL SHALL be deterministic.

9.  PEP39CL SHALL support forward compatibility.

10. PEP39CL SHALL separate identity from implementation.

11. PEP39CL SHALL detect invalid input.

12. PEP39CL SHALL be chain-agnostic.

PEP39CL SHALL NOT define chain-specific key derivation, recovery phrases
or wallet formats.

===========================================================

6.  Architecture

Generate

Random | Entropy (256 bit) | PEP39CL Encoding | Mnemonic (25 Words)

Recover

Mnemonic | PEP39CL Decoding | Entropy | PEP32 | Master Seed (256 bit) +–
BTC Adapter +– ETH Adapter +– TON Adapter +– XMR Adapter +– Future
Adapters

===========================================================

7.  Specification

Entropy Size 256 bit

Mnemonic Format 25 Words

Version 0x01

Checksum SHA-256 (8-bit checksum)

Encoding Wordlist Encoding

Seed Generation Defined by PEP32

===========================================================

8.  Encoding Flow

Entropy -> Version -> Checksum -> Word Encoding -> 25 Words

===========================================================

9.  Decoding Flow

25 Words -> Word Decoding -> Checksum Validation -> Version Validation
-> Entropy

===========================================================

10. Compatibility

PEP39CL SHALL remain blockchain independent.

PEP32 MAY derive keys through Chain Adapters.

===========================================================
