PEP32-0001 Master Seed

Version: 0.1 Draft

===========================================================

1.  Abstract

This specification defines the Master Seed architecture used by PEP32.

The Master Seed is the root secret from which chain-specific secrets may
be deterministically derived.

This specification SHALL NOT define mnemonic encoding or decoding. Those
responsibilities belong to PEP39CL.

===========================================================

2.  Goals

-   Chain Independent
-   Deterministic
-   Secure
-   Forward Compatible
-   Extensible

===========================================================

3.  Architecture

PEP39CL Mnemonic ↓ Entropy ↓ Master Seed ↓ Chain Adapter ↓
Chain-specific Secret

===========================================================

4.  Master Seed

The Master Seed is the canonical root secret of a PEP wallet.

All chain-specific secrets SHALL originate from the same Master Seed.

Only one Master Seed SHALL exist for a wallet.

===========================================================

5.  Requirements

The Master Seed SHALL:

-   Be deterministic.
-   Remain immutable.
-   Never be modified after creation.
-   Never be exposed unless explicitly exported.
-   Be suitable for deterministic derivation.

===========================================================

6.  Chain Independence

PEP32 SHALL remain chain-agnostic.

PEP32 SHALL NOT define blockchain-specific key derivation algorithms,
wallet formats or recovery phrases.

Chain-specific behavior SHALL be implemented by Chain Adapters.

===========================================================

7.  Security Considerations

Implementations SHOULD:

-   Keep the Master Seed in protected memory.
-   Erase the Master Seed from memory when no longer required.
-   Avoid logging or transmitting the Master Seed.
-   Prevent unauthorized disclosure.

===========================================================
