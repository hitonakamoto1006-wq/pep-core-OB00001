PEP32-0002 Chain Adapter Interface

Version: 0.1 Draft

===========================================================

1.  Abstract

This specification defines the standard interface between the PEP32
Master Seed and supported blockchain implementations.

PEP32 SHALL remain chain-agnostic.

This specification SHALL NOT define blockchain-specific derivation
algorithms, wallet formats or recovery phrases.

===========================================================

2.  Goals

-   Chain Independent
-   Deterministic
-   Extensible
-   Interoperable
-   Forward Compatible

===========================================================

3.  Architecture

PEP39CL Mnemonic ↓ Entropy ↓ Master Seed ↓ Chain Adapter Interface ↓
Chain-specific Implementation

===========================================================

4.  Responsibilities

A Chain Adapter SHALL:

-   Accept a Master Seed.
-   Derive a chain-specific root secret.
-   Derive accounts deterministically.
-   Derive addresses deterministically.
-   Return chain-specific public information.

===========================================================

5.  Interface

Input: - Master Seed

Output: - Chain Root Secret - Account - Address - Public Information

===========================================================

6.  Requirements

Every Chain Adapter SHALL:

-   Produce deterministic results.
-   Be independent of other adapters.
-   Never modify the Master Seed.
-   Never expose the Master Seed.
-   Support future protocol versions.

===========================================================

7.  Isolation

Compromise of one Chain Adapter SHALL NOT require exposing the Master
Seed or the internal state of other adapters.

===========================================================

8.  Compatibility

PEP32 defines only the interface.

Each blockchain SHALL implement its own adapter while conforming to this
interface.

===========================================================

9.  Security Considerations

Implementations SHOULD:

-   Protect derived secrets in memory.
-   Erase temporary secrets after use.
-   Avoid logging sensitive material.
-   Validate adapter inputs.

===========================================================
