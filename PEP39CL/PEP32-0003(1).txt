PEP32-0003 Derivation Path

Version: 0.1 Draft

===========================================================

1.  Abstract

This specification defines the logical derivation path model used by
PEP32.

The derivation path provides a deterministic hierarchy from the Master
Seed to chain-specific objects.

This specification SHALL NOT define blockchain-specific derivation
algorithms.

===========================================================

2.  Goals

-   Deterministic
-   Hierarchical
-   Chain Independent
-   Extensible
-   Forward Compatible

===========================================================

3.  Architecture

Master Seed ↓ Chain Adapter ↓ Account ↓ Address ↓ Application Object

===========================================================

4.  Derivation Levels

Level 0 : Master Seed

Level 1 : Chain

Level 2 : Account

Level 3 : Address

Level 4 : Future Extension

===========================================================

5.  Requirements

A derivation path SHALL:

-   Produce deterministic results.
-   Preserve hierarchy.
-   Never modify the Master Seed.
-   Support future path levels.

===========================================================

6.  Independence

Each Chain Adapter SHALL define its own internal derivation rules.

PEP32 defines only the logical hierarchy and SHALL remain
chain-agnostic.

===========================================================

7.  Security Considerations

Implementations SHOULD isolate derived secrets between accounts and
addresses.

Temporary secrets SHOULD be erased after use.

===========================================================

8.  Compatibility

Future specifications MAY introduce additional derivation levels.

Existing levels SHALL retain their semantic meaning.
