PEP32-0004 Extended Keys

Version: 0.1 Draft

===========================================================

1.  Abstract

This specification defines the concept of Extended Keys within PEP32.

Extended Keys provide deterministic access to a subtree of the
derivation hierarchy without exposing the Master Seed.

This specification SHALL NOT define blockchain-specific extended key
formats.

===========================================================

2.  Goals

-   Deterministic
-   Hierarchical
-   Chain Independent
-   Secure
-   Extensible

===========================================================

3.  Architecture

Master Seed ↓ Chain Adapter ↓ Extended Private Key ↓ Extended Public Key
↓ Accounts / Addresses

===========================================================

4.  Extended Key Types

Extended Private Key

-   May derive child private keys.
-   May derive child public keys.

Extended Public Key

-   SHALL derive public information only.
-   SHALL NOT derive private keys.

===========================================================

5.  Requirements

Extended Keys SHALL:

-   Preserve deterministic hierarchy.
-   Never modify the Master Seed.
-   Support future protocol versions.
-   Be suitable for watch-only implementations.

===========================================================

6.  Watch-only

Implementations MAY expose Extended Public Keys for read-only wallets.

Watch-only wallets SHALL NOT sign transactions.

===========================================================

7.  Security Considerations

Implementations SHOULD:

-   Protect Extended Private Keys.
-   Avoid exposing sensitive key material.
-   Erase temporary secrets after use.

===========================================================

8.  Compatibility

PEP32 defines only the logical concept of Extended Keys.

Blockchain-specific serialization formats are outside the scope of this
specification.

===========================================================
