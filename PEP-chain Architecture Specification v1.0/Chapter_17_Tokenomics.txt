Chapter 17. Tokenomics

17.1 Overview

The Tokenomics Layer defines the issuance, circulation, valuation and
incentive mechanisms of PEP-chain.

Core economic objects include:

-   PEP
-   CCQ
-   Mint
-   Burn
-   Reward

------------------------------------------------------------------------

17.2 PEP

PEP is the native index token of the protocol.

PEP represents the aggregate value of all Capital Contribution Quotas
(CCQs) existing within the ecosystem.

Its intrinsic valuation follows the STC principle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

17.3 CCQ

Capital Contribution Quotas (CCQs) represent proportional ownership in a
specific fund.

CCQs may be issued, transferred, redeemed and managed according to
protocol rules.

Their value is derived from NAV and STC rather than speculative price
discovery.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

17.4 Mint

Mint creates protocol assets under deterministic protocol conditions.

Typical mint events include:

-   Initial CCQ issuance
-   Fund expansion
-   Protocol-authorized issuance

Mint operations must be verifiable by every node.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

17.5 Burn

Burn permanently removes assets from circulation.

Burn may occur after protocol-defined redemption or supply management
events.

Burn decreases circulating supply while preserving deterministic
accounting.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

17.6 Reward

Rewards incentivize validators that successfully produce valid blocks.

Reward policy is protocol-defined and may include newly issued assets,
fees or other governance-approved incentives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Summary

The Tokenomics Layer aligns asset valuation, ownership and validator
incentives around deterministic economic rules rather than speculative
market mechanisms.
