Appendix

A. Glossary

PEP
    Native ecosystem index token.

CCQ
    Capital Contribution Quota.

NAV
    Net Asset Value.

STC
    Standard Token Cost (NAV / Supply).

Claim
    Validator liquidity capacity.

Ticket
    Temporary Proof of Lottery object.

PoL
    Proof of Lottery.

Validator
    Liquidity provider participating in consensus.

Redeem
    Conversion of CCQs into underlying assets.

Asset Pool
    Locked assets backing protocol liquidity.

B. Mathematics

Standard Token Cost:

STC = NAV / Supply

Claim Weight:

Weight(i) = Claim(i)

Validator Eligibility:

Claim >= Redeem Amount

Lottery Condition:

Matched Hex Suffix Length >= Difficulty

These equations define the deterministic mathematical foundation of
PEP-chain.
