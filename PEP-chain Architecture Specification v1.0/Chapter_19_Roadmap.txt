Chapter 19. Roadmap

19.1 Devnet

The Development Network validates protocol architecture, storage,
networking and Proof of Lottery in a controlled environment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

19.2 Testnet

The Testnet evaluates protocol behavior under public participation,
validator competition and realistic economic activity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

19.3 Mainnet

Mainnet represents the production deployment of PEP-chain where
canonical protocol rules govern real assets and fund settlement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

19.4 PVM

Future protocol milestones include full deployment of the PEP Virtual
Machine and Intent Programming toolchain.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

19.5 Cross-chain

Long-term objectives include interoperability with external blockchain
ecosystems through standardized cross-chain protocols.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Summary

The roadmap defines the staged evolution of PEP-chain from development
to production and future ecosystem expansion.
