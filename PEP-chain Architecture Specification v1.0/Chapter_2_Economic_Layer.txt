Chapter 2. Economic Layer

2.1 Fund Market

PEP-chain is designed as a decentralized infrastructure for the fund
market rather than a payment network. The protocol provides a settlement
layer where capital providers, liquidity providers, and fund
participants interact through deterministic economic rules.

Each fund is represented by Capital Contribution Quotas (CCQs). Users
subscribe to or redeem CCQs while the protocol manages liquidity through
validator-operated asset pools.

The economic activity of subscriptions and redeems is the primary driver
of protocol operation and Proof of Lottery participation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.2 Net Asset Value (NAV)

Net Asset Value (NAV) is the total market value of assets held by a
fund.

NAV includes all supported assets deposited into the fund, such as BTC,
ETH, stablecoins, or other approved assets.

Mathematically:

NAV = Σ(Value of all assets in the fund)

NAV is continuously updated according to market prices and represents
the intrinsic value of the fund.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.3 Standard Token Cost (STC)

PEP-chain introduces Standard Token Cost (STC) as the deterministic
pricing model for all native ecosystem assets.

STC is defined as:

STC = NAV / Supply

where: - NAV is the Net Asset Value. - Supply is the circulating supply
of the corresponding asset.

Unlike traditional cryptocurrencies, STC removes internal price
discovery for native assets. Prices are derived directly from underlying
asset value rather than speculative order books.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.4 PEP Index Token

PEP is the native index token of the ecosystem.

Rather than representing a single fund, PEP reflects the aggregate value
of all Capital Contribution Quotas existing across the protocol.

Conceptually:

PEP Value = Aggregate NAV of all CCQs / PEP Supply

PEP therefore functions as an ecosystem-wide index rather than an
individual investment vehicle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.5 Capital Contribution Quota (CCQ)

A Capital Contribution Quota (CCQ) represents proportional ownership in
a specific investment fund.

Each CCQ grants its holder exposure to the assets managed by that fund.

CCQs may be: - Issued when capital enters a fund. - Redeemed for
underlying assets. - Held as long-term investment positions. - Used
within protocol-defined economic operations.

Every CCQ derives its intrinsic value from STC.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.6 Asset Pool

An Asset Pool contains the underlying assets supplied by validators.

Validators lock assets into pools and receive non-transferable Claim
proportional to the deposited value.

When a redeem request occurs:

1.  Eligible validators are selected according to Claim.
2.  A weighted roulette chooses one validator.
3.  The selected validator fulfills the redeem.
4.  Claim is reduced by the redeemed value.
5.  The validator receives CCQs.
6.  A consensus Ticket is generated for the completed redeem.

Asset Pools therefore provide liquidity for fund settlement while
simultaneously supporting Proof of Lottery consensus.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Summary

The Economic Layer defines the intrinsic financial foundation of
PEP-chain.

Instead of relying on speculative token pricing, the protocol is built
upon measurable economic quantities including NAV, STC, Asset Pools,
Claims, and CCQs. These components collectively support decentralized
fund settlement and serve as the economic basis for the Proof of Lottery
consensus mechanism.
