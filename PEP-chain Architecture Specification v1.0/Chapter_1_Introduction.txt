Chapter 1. Introduction

1.1 Vision

PEP-chain is a Layer-1 blockchain designed for the Fund Market rather
than the payment market. Its primary mission is to become a
decentralized settlement infrastructure for tokenized investment funds,
asset pools, and liquidity providers.

The protocol introduces a new economic model in which network consensus
is derived from real liquidity activities. Validators contribute assets
to protocol pools, receive Claim as liquidity capacity, and participate
in Proof of Lottery through redeem events.

The native asset, PEP, is an index token representing the aggregate
value of Capital Contribution Quotas (CCQs) across the ecosystem.
Internal valuation follows the STC principle, where value is determined
by Net Asset Value (NAV) divided by circulating supply.

1.2 Design Philosophy

-   Economy First
-   Liquidity-backed Security
-   Deterministic Settlement
-   Intrinsic Asset Valuation
-   Modular Protocol Design
-   Long-term Upgradeability

1.3 Terminology

PEP
    Native index token of the ecosystem.

CCQ
    Capital Contribution Quota representing ownership of a fund.

NAV
    Net Asset Value.

STC
    Standard Token Cost = NAV / Supply.

Claim
    Non-transferable liquidity capacity assigned to validators.

Validator
    Liquidity provider participating in Proof of Lottery.

Redeem
    Conversion of CCQ into underlying assets.

Ticket
    Temporary consensus object created after redeem settlement.

Lottery
    Consensus mechanism deciding block production rights.

Difficulty
    Winning threshold measured by hexadecimal suffix matching.

1.4 Objectives

-   Build a decentralized settlement layer for fund markets.
-   Secure consensus using real liquidity.
-   Maintain deterministic valuation through STC.
-   Support tokenized investment products.
-   Enable programmable fund management through PVM.
-   Preserve modularity for future protocol evolution.

1.5 Non-objectives

PEP-chain is not intended to be:

-   A payment-first blockchain.
-   A Bitcoin or Ethereum clone.
-   A Proof-of-Work mining network.
-   A staking-only consensus network.
-   A speculative pricing protocol for native ecosystem assets.

Instead, PEP-chain is designed specifically for decentralized fund
settlement and Proof of Lottery consensus.
